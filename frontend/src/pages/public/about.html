Core Operational Principles

01. TRANSPARENCY

Radical Real-Time Visibility

The complainant, HOD, and facilities staff view the identical lifecycle progress simultaneously.

02. ACCOUNTABILITY

Enforced 30m Worker SLAs

No work order sits in limbo. Automatic countdown timers guarantee worker acceptance or re-routing.

03. FAIRNESS

Algorithmic Workload Balance

Dispatches prevent technician fatigue by distributing tasks evenly across certified trade personnel.

04. LOCAL JURISDICTION

Contextual Authority Scoping

Wardens only see hostel issues; Engineering HODs only see faculty laboratory work orders.

05. RESILIENCE

Offline-Ready Architecture

Technicians can view assigned work orders in basement plant rooms without continuous cellular reception.

06. COMPLIANCE

Institutional Audit Proof
