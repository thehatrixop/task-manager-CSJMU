INTERACTIVE LIFECYCLE

How CampusFlow Works

Step-by-step walkthrough of how a maintenance issue travels from initial report to certified resolution.

01

Stage 1: Complaint Creation

User Role

A student or faculty member notices an electrical spark, broken tap, or AC malfunction. They open CampusFlow, select the trade department, describe the exact room location, attach a photo, and set priority.

Output: Ticket CF-2026-00124 created with state [Pending Verification]
02

Stage 2: Authority Verification

HOD / Warden Role

The relevant HOD or Warden receives a push notification on their verification queue. They inspect the submitted photos and location. With one tap, they can approve, reject with a stated reason, or request clarification.

Output: Ticket verified by Dr. Ramesh Sharma → State changes to [Approved & Awaiting Dispatch]
03

Stage 3: Intelligent Worker Matching

Autonomous System

The CampusFlow dispatch engine activates immediately. It scans the electrical department technician roster, checks availability status, matches switchboard repair certifications, and selects the optimal worker.

Output: Selected WRK-0018 Rajesh Kumar (Score 96.4%) → State changes to [Worker Dispatched]
04

Stage 4: 30-Minute Worker Acceptance SLA

Field Worker Role

Rajesh Kumar's terminal sounds an urgent dispatch alert with a 30-minute ticking countdown. If accepted, the work order locks in. If declined or unanswered within 30 minutes, the engine automatically reassigns to the secondary standby worker.

Output: Worker accepts within 1 min 46s → State changes to [Active Job / In Progress]
05

Stage 5: Maintenance Execution & Progress Logging

Field Worker Role

The technician arrives on site, isolates power, and begins repair. The worker can submit progress logs: "Work Started", "Awaiting Materials", or "In Progress", visible in real time to the student and warden.

06

Stage 6: Verified Completion & Archival

All Roles

The technician snaps a photo of the repaired switchboard, logs parts used, and submits completion. Complainant and Warden receive notifications. The ticket is officially resolved and sealed in the immutable audit log.
